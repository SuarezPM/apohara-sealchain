apohara-sealchain receipt verifier 100% offline

Verify a apohara-sealchain receipt and its artifact entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded — content, Ed25519, and C2PA are checked locally in WebAssembly.

Drop the artifact and its .seal.json here

Powered by sealchain-wasm (Rust + wasm-bindgen + serde-wasm-bindgen). HMAC is symmetric: its secret key is never in the receipt, so it cannot be checked in the browser — this verifier says so honestly rather than faking a pass.
